Service Catalogue // Verification Standards

Services & Transparent Pricing

Every tier below is delivered under the same analytical methodology: lawful open-source collection, independent corroboration where attainable, explicit confidence rating per data point, and a structured dossier you can act on. Prices are published because guessing at cost wastes everybody's time.

METHOD // COLLECTION

Lawful OSINT Frameworks Only

Collection is limited to public records, government and corporate registries, court and property indices, published archives, and publicly accessible digital traces. We do not use pretexting, credential access, surveillance, or any data obtained unlawfully — from us or from a subcontractor.

METHOD // VERIFICATION

Rigorous Verification Standard

A finding is reported as fact only when independently corroborated by a second qualifying source. Single-source findings are reported with an explicit caveat. Pattern-only signals are flagged as leads. Unverified material is excluded from the findings section entirely.

METHOD // ETHICS

Scope & Legality Review First

Before invoicing, we review the stated objective for lawfulness and feasibility. If a matter requires licensed private investigation, falls under FCRA-regulated permissible-purpose rules, or indicates intent to harass or intimidate, we decline and say why.

Pricing Notes

Listed figures are standard engagement rates for a defined scope in a single jurisdiction. Multi-jurisdiction, multi-subject, or archival-depth work is quoted after scoping. Third-party record retrieval fees (court copies, certified documents, registry downloads) are passed through at cost and itemised. Initial scoping review is complimentary — a 20-minute call, no obligation and no card. Nothing is invoiced until you approve a written scope and fee in advance.

About the comparison figures. Each tier lists the typical market rate for comparable desk research, drawn from published 2026 industry rate surveys — not from quotes by any named firm, and not a claim about any specific competitor. We cost less because our overheads are lower: we carry no investigator's licence, run no field operations, and conduct no surveillance. We are not a licensed private investigation firm and these tiers are not a substitute for licensed investigative work — where a matter requires a licensed investigator, we say so and decline. The comparison is offered for desk research that requires no licence at all.

Founding Client Rate — First 10 Engagements

Abyssinian Intel is newly established. Our first ten engagements are taken at the founding rate shown against each tier — roughly 20% below list, applied to every option in that tier including add-ons and extended scopes. The rate is locked for the life of your case: if we are still working your matter after the tenth engagement closes, your price does not move. We are not running a countdown or a fake sale — when ten engagements have closed, list price applies and this notice comes down. What you get in exchange for an early bet on us is the same scope cap, the same written estimate, and the same verification standard as any later client.

Where an Automated Report Wins

Speed and price, and it is legally required for FCRA-governed decisions — tenancy, employment, credit, insurance. If that is your decision, buy the compliant report first. Ours adds context beside it; it never replaces it, and we will tell you so rather than take the work.

Where a Licensed Investigator Wins

Surveillance, field interviews, service of process, sworn testimony, and anything a licence is required for. We do none of these and cannot be substituted for them. When your matter needs one, we say so at the scoping call and decline the engagement — before any fee is discussed.
